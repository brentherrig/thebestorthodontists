Questions

How is this list built?

Every orthodontic practice inside Chesapeake is scored on its Google rating and review count, weighted so a few reviews cannot beat hundreds, plus credit for an AAO locator listing and an ABO board-certified orthodontist at the practice. The top ten are listed. The full method is on the methodology page.

Can a practice pay to be on the list?

No. There is no paid placement, sponsorship or submission. The list comes from public data only.

How current is it?

Ratings and review counts were pulled on October 16, 2026. The list is rebuilt from fresh data, and the date on the page changes when it is.

How do I correct a listing?

Email the address on the corrections page with the practice name and what to change.

The Best Orthodontists is a CCA Marketing project. Lists are built from public data: Google ratings and review counts, the American Association of Orthodontists locator and the American Board of Orthodontics locator.
